Section C — Case Studies

Question 21 — Case Study: The School Garden
Case Study:
A school creates a circular garden. The garden has a radius of 5 metres. Students measure the plants each week and record their observations. They also notice that some plants grow better when they receive more sunlight.

a) Calculate the diameter of the garden.

b) Why is sunlight important for plants?

c) Give one variable that students should keep the same when comparing plant growth.

Question 22 — Case Study: The Science Laboratory
Case Study:
A student has three unknown liquids. Liquid A has a pH of 2, Liquid B has a pH of 7 and Liquid C has a pH of 10. The student uses an indicator to investigate the liquids.

a) Which liquid is acidic?

b) Which liquid is neutral?

c) Which liquid is alkaline?

d) What does the pH scale tell us?

Question 23 — Case Study: Community Responsibility
Case Study:
A group of students notices that rubbish is being left around their school. They organise a clean-up campaign. They divide students into groups, make posters and encourage everyone to put rubbish in the correct bins.

a) Name one UAE/community value shown by the students.

b) Why is cooperation important in this situation?

c) Give one way the students could encourage the campaign to continue.

Question 24 — Case Study: Electricity
Case Study:
A student builds a circuit using a cell, wires, a switch and a bulb. When the switch is closed, the bulb lights up. When the switch is opened, the bulb goes off.

a) Why does the bulb light when the switch is closed?

b) What happens to the circuit when the switch is opened?

c) Name one material that could be used as a conductor.
