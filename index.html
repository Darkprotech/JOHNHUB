⭕ Circles

Radius

The distance from the centre of a circle to its edge.

Diameter

The distance across a circle through its centre.

Diameter = 2 × Radius

Circumference

C = 2πr

Where r is the radius.

Area

Area = πr²

Example

If a circle has a radius of 7 cm:

Diameter = 2 × 7 = 14 cm
Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 cm

📏 Constructions

Important constructions

  • Constructing a 90° angle
  • Constructing a 45° angle
  • Constructing an angle bisector
  • Constructing a perpendicular bisector

Equipment

  • Compass
  • Ruler
  • Pencil
  • Protractor when required

An angle bisector divides an angle into two equal angles.

A perpendicular bisector crosses a line segment at 90° and divides it into two equal parts.

❓ Finding the Unknown

When solving equations, use inverse operations to isolate the unknown.

x + 5 = 12

Subtract 5 from both sides:

x = 7
3x = 18

Divide both sides by 3:

x = 6

📦 Area & Perimeter

Rectangle

Area = Length × Width
Perimeter = 2(Length + Width)

Square

Area = Side × Side
Perimeter = 4 × Side

Triangle

Area = ½ × Base × Height

📊 Mean, Median & Range

Mean

Add all values and divide by the number of values.

Mean = Total ÷ Number of values

Median

Put the numbers in order and find the middle value.

Range

Range = Highest value − Lowest value

🧪 Acids, Bases & Neutrals

The pH scale is used to describe how acidic or alkaline a substance is.

  • pH below 7 → acidic
  • pH 7 → neutral
  • pH above 7 → alkaline

Examples

  • Lemon juice → acidic
  • Vinegar → acidic
  • Pure water → neutral
  • Soap solution → alkaline

Indicators

Indicators are substances that change colour depending on whether a solution is acidic or alkaline.

Neutralisation

Neutralisation happens when an acid reacts with a base, producing substances with a more neutral pH.

💡 Light & Reflection

Luminous objects

Objects that produce their own light are luminous. The Sun and a light bulb are examples.

Non-luminous objects

These objects do not produce their own light. We see them because light reflects from them.

Reflection

Reflection occurs when light bounces off a surface.

Law of Reflection

Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection

🌑 Shadows

A shadow forms when an opaque object blocks light.

A basic shadow experiment needs:

  • Light source
  • Object
  • Screen

Changing the distance between the light source and object can change the size of the shadow.

Fair Tests

A fair test changes one variable while keeping other important variables the same.

⚡ Electricity & Circuits

Conductors

Materials that allow electric current to pass through them. Copper is a common conductor.

Insulators

Materials that do not allow electric current to pass through easily. Rubber and plastic are examples.

Complete Circuit

A complete circuit provides a continuous path for electric current.

Switches

A closed switch completes the circuit. An open switch breaks the circuit.

🫀 Human Digestive System

Mouth / Buccal Cavity

Food is chewed and mixed with saliva.

Oesophagus

Moves food from the mouth towards the stomach.

Stomach

Churns food and mixes it with digestive juices.

Small Intestine

Most nutrient absorption takes place here.

Large Intestine

Absorbs much of the remaining water.

Rectum and Anus

Store and remove waste from the body.

🇮🇳 Constitution of India

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of India. It establishes the framework for government and describes important rights and duties.

Preamble

The Preamble explains important ideals and objectives of the Constitution.

Fundamental Rights

Fundamental Rights protect important freedoms and equality guaranteed by the Constitution.

Fundamental Duties

Fundamental Duties describe responsibilities citizens are expected to follow.

🏛️ Three Branches of Government

Legislature

Makes laws.

Executive

Implements and carries out laws and government decisions.

Judiciary

Interprets laws and resolves legal disputes.

🗳️ Democracy

Democracy is a system of government in which people participate in choosing representatives and in public decision-making.

Important ideas

  • Equality
  • Participation
  • Rights
  • Responsibilities
  • Rule of law

🇦🇪 UAE Values

Respect

Treating others with consideration and dignity.

Tolerance

Respecting people who may have different backgrounds or views.

Responsibility

Being accountable for your actions.

Cooperation

Working together to achieve a common goal.

Empathy

Trying to understand another person's feelings or situation.

Community

People working together and supporting one another.

📚 Important Vocabulary

  • Constitution: Fundamental rules and principles for governing a country.
  • Democracy: A system in which people participate in government.
  • Rights: Freedoms and protections people are entitled to.
  • Duty: A responsibility that a person is expected to fulfil.
  • Legislature: The law-making branch of government.
  • Judiciary: The branch responsible for interpreting laws and resolving disputes.
  • Executive: The branch responsible for implementing laws and government decisions.
